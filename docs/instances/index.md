# Ethos Types

## By Naturality

There are two types of ethos and a mixing of the two:

1. Natural Ethos
2. Mixed Ethos
3. Synthetic Ethos

### Natural Ethos

1. Nuclear Family
1. Extended Family
1. Family Legacy
1. War Band
1. Tribe
1. Tribal Legacy, Ancestral Cult
1. Phratry
1. Ethnic Group

### Mixed Ethos

1. Class of Children
1. Monastic Community
1. Esprit du Corps
1. Mafia, Crime Syndicate
1. Army
1. Organized Religion

### Synthetic Ethos

1. Social Contract
1. State
1. Rule of Law
1. Corporation
1. Syndicate
1. Political Party
1. Occult Society, Freemasonry
1. School of Thought
1. Ethos by Design
