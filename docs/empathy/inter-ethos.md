## Inter-Ethos Framework

### Identification

1. **Distinct existence — `ack_other_ethos`**  
   I acknowledge that another ethos and its bearers exist distinctly from mine.

2. **Representational integrity — `other_ethos_self_representation`**  
   I describe another ethos using its public declarations and authorized representatives, distinguish its versions and internal diversity, permit correction of material misrepresentation, and do not claim to represent it without authorization.

### Dignity, status, and liberty

3. **Equal bearer dignity — `equal_other_ethos_bearer_dignity`**  
   I accord persons equal basic moral worth regardless of the ethos they affirm, reject, join, change, or leave.

4. **Equal civic standing — `equal_other_ethos_civic_standing`**  
   I accord comparably situated persons and peaceful institutions equal legal protection, access, responsibilities, and due process regardless of ethos identity.

5. **Voluntary affiliation — `voluntary_ethos_affiliation`**  
   I do not compel anyone to adopt, profess, finance, retain, conceal, abandon, or leave an ethos through force, threats, punitive disabilities, deception, or exploitation of dependency.

6. **Liberty of peaceful practice — `liberty_other_ethos_practice`**  
   I permit another ethos’s bearers to express, teach, assemble, associate, choose representatives, and govern their voluntary internal affairs, subject to publicly stated harm rules applied impartially.

7. **Protection and remedy — `protect_other_ethos`**  
   Where I bear responsibility, I protect other ethoses and their bearers from targeted violence, coercion, and discrimination, and provide remedy for attributable harm.

### Participation and communication

8. **Affected-party voice — `affected_ethos_voice`**  
   When my decisions materially affect another ethos, I give its affected bearers or authorized representatives a meaningful opportunity to present their interests, receive reasons, contest evidence, and appeal.

9. **Reciprocal dialogue — `interethos_dialogue`**  
   I make voluntary, good-faith dialogue possible in which each party may listen, question, answer, criticize, disagree, revise, and withdraw without punishment.

### Consideration and evaluation

10. **Equal initial consideration — `equal_initial_ethos_consideration`**  
    I do not discount another ethos’s relevant interests or reasons merely because of its identity; unequal conclusions require relevant public reasons.

11. **Evidential parity — `interethos_evidential_parity`**  
    I apply the same burdens of proof and evidential standards to comparable claims from my ethos and another ethos.

12. **Ethical-value assessment — `other_ethos_value_assessment`**  
    I identify separately the goods, harms, virtues, and defects I attribute to another ethos and provide reasons for each assessment.

13. **Claim-specific truth relation — `other_ethos_truth_relation`**  
    I assess material claims individually as supported, unsupported, false, undecided, or incommensurable rather than declaring an entire ethos equally true or false.

### Authority and action

14. **Scoped authority recognition — `other_ethos_authority_scope`**  
    For any authority I recognize, I identify its source, representatives, subjects, domain, duration, and means of review. Authority over voluntary adherents does not automatically extend to outsiders.

15. **Practical cooperation — `interethos_cooperation`**  
    I may cooperate with another ethos on specified compatible actions without merging our ethoses, concealing disagreement, or endorsing all its claims.

16. **Conflict resolution and repair — `interethos_conflict_resolution`**  
    When our claims or actions conflict, I use a public process for negotiation, mediation, adjudication, appeal, and proportionate repair rather than coercive suppression.

The most important corrections are:

- **Dignity belongs to persons**, not propositions.
- **Civic equality does not imply equal truth.**
- **Equal evidential treatment does not require equal conclusions.**
- **Truth and authority are independent:** a claim may be true without binding anyone, while an institution may possess limited authority without being ethical.
- “Equally true or authoritative” should therefore be removed as a single criterion. Whole, mutually contradictory ethoses cannot be equally true in the same respect without conflicting with `no_contradiction_claim`.

----


-- In the existing taxonomy, `ack_other_ethos` corresponds to item 1; `ack_other_ethos_importance` should become item 10; `impartial` and `proof_accepting` support item 11; `challengeable` and `constructive` support item 9; `alignable` supports item 15; and `arbitrage` supports item 16. Items 2–8 and 14 are substantially new.
